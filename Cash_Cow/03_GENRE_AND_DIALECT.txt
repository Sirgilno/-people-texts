CASH COW — GENRE / DIALECT / SUNO TRIGGER MAP
Source of truth for Style of Music and lyric dialect. Genre tag ALWAYS first.

MOOD-FIRST (silent, every project)
1) Name the mood in one sentence (wholesome family comedy / betrayal / gym fail / toddler chaos / slow-burn romance / etc.).
2) Scan this FULL map — do not default to the same 2–3 genres.
3) Justify internally by emotional DNA, not just trigger words.
4) Track genres already used in this chat. Repeat only if the mood genuinely repeats. If you reuse because it is truly the best fit, say so in one aside to the user.
5) Option 2 = unrelated genre family; contrast should be interesting, not lazy-random.

CRITICAL LANGUAGE
English is the base of original sections (V2 / Chorus / V3 / Outro). Dialect words there are seasoning.
Every Verse 2, Chorus, Verse 3, and Outro MUST contain ≥1 full standalone line in the home language of the genre.
That home-language line MUST end-rhyme with the line before it or the line after it. End rhyme = last stressed vowel + all following sounds match when spoken. Adjacent is not a rhyme. Spelling is not a rhyme. Use any dictionary word (English or that language) that matches THIS line's last sound and fits the song. No template words.
A dumped Hindi / Zulu / Spanish / Arabic / Pidgin sentence with no sonic partner is a fail even if the FINAL CHECK box is ticked.

If the genre is not listed: infer home culture, specific instrument names, BPM, vocal delivery; same specificity.

RAP OVERRIDE
UK Drill, Chicago/NY Drill, Grime, Hip-Hop, Trap, or any rap variant: hard original bars at the craft level of Kendrick Lamar, Eminem, Lil Wayne, Ludacris, and Nas.
Those names are INTERNAL ONLY. Never put them in lyrics. Never put them in Style of Music. Never write "in the style of".
Required: punchlines, double entendres, metaphors inside metaphors, multisyllabic / internal rhymes, punch word last, setup-then-flip.
Slang seasoning (mandem, bruv, no cap, wasteman, opp) is not a substitute. If there is no "wait..." moment, the bar failed.
Keep the clip's vibe (funny / cheerful / sad / horror-comedy). Do not flatten the story into generic gangsta.

AMAPIANO / AFRO HOUSE / GQOM
Triggers: Amapiano, log drum bass, log drum bassline, deep synth sub, jazzy piano chords, shaker loops, smooth male vocals in English and Zulu, Zulu ad libs and chants, late-night SA groove, township energy, 112-115 BPM.
Seasoning: eish, sharp sharp, yebo, hawu, sana, mara, lekker, joh, haibo, danko, grootman, ntwana, phola, moja, zaka, vosho, kasi, jaiva, ziyakhala, waya waya.
Gqom extra: wololo, ziyasha, asambe, eThekwini, Umlazi, shay'izandla, hlanya.
Full line: Zulu (or Tswana). Tags: Danko grootman | Ziyakhala ke | Vula circle | Kasi vibe
Example: Yanos iyachisa, asijayive sbali, log drum ikhala kamnandi e-straata.

AFROBEATS / AFROPOP
Triggers: Afrobeat, Afropop, syncopated African percussion, Yoruba and English male vocals, call-and-response, Lagos nightclub energy, shaker rhythm, rimshot snare, 102-108 BPM.
Seasoning: e don do, wetin, wahala, omo, sabi, ehen, abeg, shey, chop, waka, gbedu, japa, kolo, no cap, odogwu, jeje.
Ghana Twi extra: chale, sika, odo, onyame, yɛnkɔ, gye Nyame, bokoor.
Full line: Nigerian Pidgin (or Twi). Tags: No wahala | Chop life | Gbedu wey dey burst brain
Example: I dey my corner, I dey chop life jeje, no wahala dey near me.

DANCEHALL / REGGAE
Triggers: Dancehall, digital riddim, energetic, patois vocals, bass-heavy, offbeat skanking guitar, toasting delivery, raspy male vocals, 90-105 BPM.
Seasoning: gyal, bredda, pull up, wheel, selecta, wine, bruk out, big up, badmind, riddim, likkle, yute, wah gwaan, nuh, dem, mash up, run di place.
Full line: Jamaican Patois. Tags: Wah gwaan star | Wheel and come again | Run di place
Example: Selecta, wheel and come again make we shell di place.

ROOTS REGGAE
Triggers: Roots reggae, offbeat guitar, one drop drums, conscious lyrics, deep bass, warm spiritual male vocals, 70-85 BPM.
Seasoning: I and I, Jah, Babylon, Zion, livity, overstand, ital, seen, bredrin, give thanks, chant down, word sound and power.
Full line: Conscious Patois. Tags: Satta Massagana | Chant down Babylon | Give thanks and praises
Example: I and I trod through di valley, chanting down di downpressor with word, sound and power.

REGGAETON / LATIN POP

Seasoning: perreo, dale, oye, papi, mami, fuego, corazon, flow, corillo, gata, dure, nos fuimos, parce, chimba, prender.
Full line: Spanish. Tags: ¡Duro! | Hasta abajo | Sube el dembow
Example: Ella se viste acicalá pa' ir al jangueo, le mete duro al perreo cuando siente el dembow.

BOSSA NOVA / SAMBA
Triggers: Bossa nova jazz, nylon string guitar, soft brushed percussion, warm bass, smooth Brazilian vocals, 85-100 BPM. Samba: bateria, cavaquinho, pandeiro, surdo, festive street energy.
Seasoning: saudade, amor, meu bem, coração, carioca, sorriso, brisa, mar, flor, ginga, morro, festa, alegria.
Full line: Portuguese. Tags: Cheia de graça | Deixa o barquinho correr | Pé no chão
Example: Olha que coisa mais linda, mais cheia de graça, é ela menina que vem e que passa.

MEDITERRANEAN / GREEK / TURKISH

UK DRILL / GRIME

CHICAGO / NY DRILL

HARD BARS REQUIRED. Keep campaign-safe unless comments themselves go darker — then still no glorifying real violence against minors.

TRAP / HIP-HOP / RAP VARIANTS
Genre tag first. Hard original bars at the Kendrick / Eminem / Wayne / Ludacris / Nas craft ceiling. Punchlines last. Callbacks. Double entendres. Multis.
Those names never appear in lyrics or Style of Music. No "in the style of".

Seasoning: eerie English. If the comments are Arabic (or another language), the home-language line is in THAT language — this genre is atmosphere, not a license to translate Verse 1.

Seasoning: English rock cadence. HARD BARS if rap-sung (same RAP OVERRIDE).
