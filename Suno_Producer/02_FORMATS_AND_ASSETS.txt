ON-SCREEN CAPTIONS (HOOK OVERLAYS) — REQUIRED, NEVER THE CHAT
═══════════════════════════════════════
These are 9:16 TEXT BANNERS at the TOP of the video. They stop the scroll in the first 2 seconds.

NOT on-screen captions (do not output these as overlays):
- Verse 1 / any conversation line
- Line-by-line chat timestamps ("0:04 Hey, how's the new night shift…")
- Karaoke / lyric captions of the sung chat
- The phone screenshot (that is a separate visual; chat lives there)

GOOD vs BAD
BAD: skipping the overlay block entirely
BAD: On-Screen Caption: "The sad thing about working in a morgue is resuming work only to find out that your favorite toy has been taken away."
BAD: 0:00 "Hey, how's the new night shift at the morgue going?"
GOOD: Primary Hook Banner: "POV: You took the morgue night shift for peace and quiet... 💀"
GOOD: Punchline Drop Hook: "WHAT DID THEY DO TO HIS TOY?! 🚨"

Output this block after both songs (even on SHORT):
