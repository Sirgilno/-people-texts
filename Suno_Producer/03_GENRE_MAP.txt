SUNO PRODUCER — GENRE / DIALECT / TRIGGER MAP
Source of truth for Style of Music prompts and lyric dialect. Genre tag ALWAYS first.

MOOD-FIRST (silent)
1) Read Verse 1. That is the conversation. Who is talking, and what is the tone?
2) Feel it, then pick from this map. The map is a menu, not a default and not a lock.
3) Option 2 = a different family that still fits THIS same chat.
4) If the Style would sound wrong under this screenshot, pick again.
5) If trap/hip-hop/drill truly fits this chat: multi-syllable rhymes, punch word last.

FOREIGN LINE RULE
Every Verse 2, Verse 3, and Outro needs ≥1 full standalone line in the genre's home language.

MAP
- Amapiano / Afro House / Gqom → Triggers: Amapiano, log drum bass, deep synth sub, jazzy piano chords, shaker loops, smooth male vocals English and Zulu, Zulu ad libs, township energy, 112-115 BPM. Lyrics seasoning: eish, sharp sharp, yebo, hawu, sana, mara, lekker, joh, haibo. Full Zulu line required in V2/V3/Outro.
- Afrobeats / Afropop → Afrobeat, Afropop, syncopated percussion, Yoruba and English vocals, Lagos energy, rimshot snare, 102-108 BPM. Pidgin: e don do, wetin, wahala, omo, sabi, abeg, shey. Full Pidgin line required.
- Dancehall / Reggae → Dancehall, digital riddim, patois vocals, bass-heavy, offbeat skank, 90-105 BPM. Patois: di, dem, seh, fi, weh, likkle, pon, nuh, riddim, bwoy. Full Patois line required.
- Roots Reggae → Roots reggae, one drop, deep bass, conscious warm vocals, 70-85 BPM.
- Reggaeton / Latin Pop → Reggaeton, dembow, punchy 808, tropical synths, 92-100 BPM. Spanish seasoning + one full Spanish line.
- Bossa Nova / Samba → Bossa nova jazz, nylon guitar, brushed percussion, Brazilian vocals, 85-100 BPM. Portuguese + one full Portuguese line.
- Mediterranean / Greek / Turkish → oud, bouzouki, hand percussion, passionate vocals, 95-110 BPM. One full Greek or Turkish line.
- Flamenco → Spanish guitar, handclaps, cajon, duende, 100-120 BPM. One full Spanish line.
- Bhangra / Bollywood → Bhangra EDM, dhol, brass, Punjabi energy, 128 BPM. One full Punjabi-flavored line.
- J-Pop → bright synths, precise vocals, anime aesthetic, 110-130 BPM. One full Japanese line.
- K-Pop → polished production, Korean+English, idol energy, 100-130 BPM. One full Korean line.
- Sea Shanty / Celtic Folk → tin whistle, fiddle, bodhran, communal vocals OR sea shanty call-and-response. One full chant-style line in that tradition.
- French Chanson → breathy vocals, acoustic/piano, cafe atmosphere, 80-100 BPM. One full French line.
- Cumbia / Vallenato → accordion, bongos, Colombian coastal energy, 85-100 BPM. One full Spanish line.
- Highlife / Juju → West African guitars, brass, call-and-response, 100-115 BPM. One full Pidgin/Twi-flavored line.
- Fado → saudade, nylon guitar, mournful vocals, 60-80 BPM. One full Portuguese line.
- UK Drill / Grime → UK drill, sliding 808s, cold London vocals, 140-145 BPM. mandem, ting, wasteman, innit, bruv, peak. Hard multi-syllable bars.
- Chicago / NY Drill → aggressive deadpan, trap hats, heavy 808, 140-150 BPM. Hard American drill bars.
- Country / Bluegrass → Southern twang, acoustic, pedal steel, storytelling, 85-100 BPM.
- Blues → Delta/slow blues, slide guitar, gritty vocals, 60-80 BPM. Home language: English.
- Cinematic Trap → dark cinematic trap, minor-key piano, deep 808s, sparse hats, deadpan male vocals, horror-comedy atmosphere, 130-140 BPM. Home language: English. Hard original bars; punchlines last.
- Phonk → phonk, cowbell, drifted 808s, dark bass, chopped vocal stabs, night-drive atmosphere, 130-140 BPM. Home language: English.
- 90s Grunge → 90s grunge, distorted guitar, dropping drums, raw male vocals, 70-90 BPM. Home language: English.
- Mariachi → brass, guitarron, vihuela, proud Mexican vocals, 100-120 BPM. One full Spanish line.
- Gospel Pop → choir, organ, Southern belt, 80-100 BPM.
- Soul / R&B → warm emotive vocals, groove, 75-95 BPM.
- Stadium Anthem → crowd chant, massive choir, euphoric build, brass fanfare, 100-120 BPM. NO FIFA / World Cup / player names.
- Trap / Hip-Hop / Rap variants → genre tag first; hard original bars; punchlines last; callbacks. Home language: English.

If genre not listed: infer home culture, specific instruments, BPM, vocal delivery; same specificity standard.
